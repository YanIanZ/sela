## Overall Impression

A real design, not a template with the logo swapped. The conviction is in the restraint. The biggest opportunity is the moment after sign-in: Home gives a returning user the poster they just read and pushes their own continuity below the fold.

## What's Working

- The safety pair never moves: Help and Quick exit in the same header position on every screen, /help lists verified services rather than routing through the model.
- Honest, non-clinical voice: "Kamu menentukan apa yang disimpan dan apakah balasan AI diizinkan", "Balasan AI tidak diizinkan. Tulisanmu tetap di sini.", footer "Sela bukan layanan darurat dan tidak memberi diagnosis."
- The empty state teaches the thesis: "Tulisanmu tidak harus menjadi kesimpulan."
