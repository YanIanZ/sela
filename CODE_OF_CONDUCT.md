# Code of Conduct

Sela exists so that people can be heard without judgement. We hold the people who build it, and the people who use its community space, to the same standard.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Sela project and its community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, mental health, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially when someone is struggling
- Listening first, and offering support or a different view without pressure
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Protecting other people's privacy

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Bullying, harassment, insults, mockery or deliberate intimidation, in public or in private
- Hate speech or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or mental health
- Encouraging self-harm, suicide or violence, or minimising someone's distress
- Sexual language, imagery or advances, and any sexual content involving minors
- Sharing someone's personal information, story or screenshots without explicit permission (doxxing)
- Posing as a clinician, giving diagnoses or medication advice
- Spam, scams, recruiting or soliciting contact
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Community space in the app

Sela's anonymous peer-sharing space follows this code too. Posts and replies are checked by the safety router and by Meta moderation before they are shown; content that bullies, harasses or endangers someone is hidden at once and reviewed by an operator. Anyone can report a post or reply. If something in the community suggests a person may be in danger, Sela shows them help — Sela does not contact anyone on their behalf.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chats and the in-app community — and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/YanIanZ/sela/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [YanIanZ](https://github.com/YanIanZ); in the app, use **Report** on the post or reply. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, following these guidelines:

1. **Correction** — a private, written explanation of the harm and a request to change.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the project or community for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations, harassment, or harm to a person in distress.
