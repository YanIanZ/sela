# Blockers

Updated: 2026-10-26 (owner brief v3; B-8 to B-15 added from the PM decisions of 2026-10-26). Only **external** blockers are listed: things that cannot be completed with the access available in this environment. Each states what is blocked, why, the evidence, what was done instead, and the exact owner action that unblocks it. Independent work continues around every blocker (owner brief §6: separate feature implementation from launch requirements).

Blockers are grouped by what they stop:

- **A. Implementation and verification blockers** stop a feature from being finished or from reaching "verified live". Everything else in that feature is built and verified locally against labelled test doubles.
- **B. Launch blockers** stop a controlled pilot with real users or a public release. They do not stop implementation.
- **C. Hackathon submission** blockers concern the Meta event only.

Statements about laws are the repository's reading of official texts fetched on 2026-10-26, not legal advice.
