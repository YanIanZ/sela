## What this is, and what it is not

- **One row per v3 feature.** Each row lists the register records that bear on it, with their machine design label and their age band, the counter-evidence, the claim boundary, the declared gaps, and the v3 gap-search candidates queued for human screening.
- **Not appraisal.** No record was read in full or assessed for risk of bias by a person. The register records keep `reviewer_status: not_reviewed`; their inclusion is the inherited title/abstract screening. The v3 candidates are machine-screened only (`sela-v3-tiab-screen-v1.2`) and are reading lists, not evidence.
- **Adults and minors are kept apart.** A record supports something for ages 15-17 only if its machine age band is `includes_15_17_explicit`: the age range the pipeline accepted for a primary study (one consistent explicit full-text range, not a review's eligibility rule, not one of several conflicting candidates, not an advisory group's ages) overlaps 15-17. Even then it holds only within its own limits. Records with a minors signal but no such range appear in the minors row as context with ages unverified. Adult evidence is never cited for minors mode.
- **Associations are not effects.** Cohorts, cross-sectional surveys and secondary analyses are read as associations, whatever their abstracts conclude.
- **Product use.** These rows inform product decisions and educational copy. They are not citations to attach to empathic replies, and none of them shows that Sela works or is safe.

> One row per v3 feature. 'register_support' lists records of the 100-record register (title/abstract screened v0, machine-verified metadata); relation 'direct' = the record studies this feature's question, 'adjacent' = a neighbouring question, 'indirect' = context only.
> Design and population columns are machine labels (study_type, age_band) from the corpus; they are unreviewed. Adult and adolescent evidence are kept apart: only records with age band includes_15_17_explicit (the accepted, explicit age range of a primary study overlaps 15-17) can speak about ages 15-17. Records with conflicting explicit ranges, a review whose eligibility includes minors, or youth words without ages carry a minors signal only; in the minors row they are listed as context with ages unverified, never as evidence about 15-17-year-olds.
> 'counter_evidence' lists records that cut against the feature or limit what it may claim.
> 'v3 candidates' come from the new frozen gap search and are machine-screened only; they are reading lists for people, not evidence.
> Associations (cohorts, cross-sectional, secondary analyses) are never read as causal effects.
> Nothing here is appraisal: no record was read in full or assessed for risk of bias by a person (blocker B-4).
> 'topics' on a register record says which brief section 9 topic it is evidence for; a record of a row with several brief topics counts only for the topics it is tagged with, and an empty list means context for the feature only. The 'Brief section 9 topics' table is built from these tags, never from the union of a row's records.
> 'inference' (agent-drafted, unreviewed) says what kind of reading a record allows here, for example 'mediation model on cross-sectional data: associations only, not causal pathways'. 'note' carries the agent's reading of the public abstract where the machine fields are silent (for example ages stated in an abstract that have no machine-verified range).
> Every row states its age groups: ages 15-17 included (accepted explicit range), minors signal with ages unverified, adults only, ages not stated. Evidence from one group is not read as evidence for another.
